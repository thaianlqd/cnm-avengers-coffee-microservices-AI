## Final image generation prompts

### Hero

Use case: photorealistic-natural, premium coffee brand website hero photograph. Create a wide landscape editorial photograph for an original Vietnamese brand called Avengers Coffee whose brand palette is deep coffee red (#B22830) and crisp white. A close, inviting cafe table scene: a red ceramic cup and saucer holding a hot latte with natural imperfect rosetta foam, a clear glass of Vietnamese iced milk coffee with beautiful real ice and condensed milk swirls, and a small stainless-steel Vietnamese phin nearby. Smooth ivory stone table, rich deep red upholstered cafe booth and softly lit white plaster wall in the background. Morning sunlight from the side, tactile ceramic and condensation, authentic photographic detail, warm but neutral light, realistic scale. Compose the cups in the lower-right half, leave the left third airy with softly textured pale wall and gentle shadows for large red HTML headline overlay. Elegant cinematic hospitality photography, understated and contemporary, organic asymmetry, no people, no decorative coffee bean scatter, no artificial splashes, no excessive props. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark. Image should work as a full-width 16:9 website hero with a clean red-and-white art direction.

### Tea and pastry

Use case: photorealistic-natural editorial food photograph for Avengers Coffee, an original Vietnamese coffee shop website with a deep red (#B22830) and white brand palette. A beautiful clear glass of peach-orange-lemongrass iced tea sits beside a flaky golden croissant on a small white ceramic plate, atop a crisp white cafe table. A ripe peach wedge and one orange slice naturally garnish the drink, real condensation and irregular ice. Light from a nearby window makes gentle realistic shadows, a deep red linen napkin provides the only bold color accent. Uncluttered bright white background, considered contemporary hospitality photography with tactile crumb and glass detail, not a glossy CGI advertising render. Medium-wide framing, entire glass and pastry visible, approximately 3:2 landscape composition. No lettering, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no flowers, no floating ingredients.
